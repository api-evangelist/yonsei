Title: ATP release mediated by TRPM3 enhances invasion in glioblastoma Authors: Bae, Kkot Garam; Song, Chae Won; Yoo, Jae Hong; Lee, Myunghoon; Koo, Noah; Lee, Gangsan; Park, Yongmin Mason; Yoo, Jihwan; Hwang, In-Young; Woo, Dong Ho; Lee, C. Justin; Han, Kyung-Seok Abstract: Glioblastoma (GBM) is the most aggressive and lethal form of primary brain tumor, characterized by uncontrolled proliferation and invasion into surrounding brain tissue. Mechanical stimulation (MS) in the tumor microenvironment (TME) has been correlated to tumor progression, partly via ATP release.
